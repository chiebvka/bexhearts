## 1. The service
Bexhearts helps couples grow together in faith: devotionals, shared and personal prayers, check-ins, boundaries, dates, a shared journal, streaks, and points. It is a devotional tool — **not** professional counseling, therapy, medical, or crisis services, and not a substitute for your church community. If you are in danger or crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line.

## 2. Accounts & eligibility
- You must be 17+ and provide accurate information.
- One account per person. You're responsible for keeping your credentials secure.
- Sign-in via email, Apple, or Google; third-party sign-in is also governed by that provider's terms.

## 3. Couples, linking & shared content
- Bexhearts is built around a two-person couple space created by invite code.
- Content you place in shared spaces (shared prayers, check-ins, journal, dates, boundaries) is visible to your partner and remains available to them even if you later leave — see §8.
- Personal prayers stay private to you.
- You confirm you have your partner's agreement to share couple content (including photos of them) within the app.

## 4. Subscriptions & billing
- **Using Bexhearts requires an active subscription or free trial.** Every couple gets a 3-day free trial; the trial requires a payment method and converts to a paid subscription unless cancelled before it ends. Signing in, completing onboarding (including linking with your partner), restoring purchases, and deleting your account are always available without a subscription.
- **One subscription covers both partners.** Entitlement is held at the couple level: whichever of you subscribes, both of you get access, and it stays with the couple.
- Bexhearts uses a free trial followed by a paid subscription, **billed per couple**: one partner subscribes; the linked partner inherits access without a second charge.
- Payment runs through Apple App Store / Google Play; manage or cancel there. Trials convert automatically unless cancelled before the trial ends.
- "Restore Purchases" is available in the app. Refunds follow Apple/Google policy.
- Deleting your account does **not** cancel your subscription — cancel in your store settings (the app reminds you at deletion).
- If the subscribing partner leaves the couple, premium persists until the current period ends, after which the remaining partner is prompted to subscribe.

## 5. AI-composed prayers
- Optional and consent-gated. AI output can be imperfect; review a composed prayer before praying it, and treat it as an aid, not doctrine or advice.
- Attempting to use the composer for content other than genuine prayer requests (§6) may result in the feature or account being restricted.
- **Fair use:** composition is generously rate-limited per couple (currently 40 compositions per day and 150 per month) to keep the feature sustainable for everyone. Limits may be adjusted; already-composed prayers always remain viewable.

## 6. Acceptable use — and what gets you banned
You may not:
- harass, abuse, threaten, or harm your partner or anyone else through the app;
- upload unlawful content, sexual content involving minors (zero tolerance; reported to authorities), or content infringing others' rights;
- misuse the AI composer (attempts to generate hateful, sexual, or non-prayer content);
- **game the points system** — automation, scripted activity, fake accounts, self-linking multiple accounts you control, or any scheme to inflate points or leaderboard standing;
- circumvent billing, share accounts, scrape the service, or probe its security;
- impersonate others or misrepresent your identity to the rewards program.

**Enforcement:** we may warn, remove content, freeze points, disqualify from rewards, suspend, or permanently ban — at our discretion, with notice where practicable. **Effect on your partner:** enforcement targets the offending account; your partner keeps their account and the couple content they own. Where the *couple* benefited from abuse (e.g. farmed points), couple-level points may be adjusted or reset. A banned subscriber's partner retains access until the paid period ends.

## 7. Points, leaderboard & future rewards
- Points reflect in-app activity (prayer sessions, devotionals, check-ins, journal entries, dates). Values may change; points have **no cash value**, are non-transferable, and may be recalculated to correct errors or abuse.
- The leaderboard shows your couple pseudonymously (masked names + couple number + country flag) unless you opt in to show first names; either partner can opt out at any time.
- **Rewards program (announced intent, not yet offered):** any future prize draw or reward will have its own official rules published before it opens. Baseline eligibility we currently anticipate (subject to the official rules and law): an active paid subscription in good standing with a minimum tenure [e.g. 3 consecutive months]; a linked couple with **both** partners genuinely active [e.g. activity in ≥ N of the last 30 days]; no enforcement actions; identity + age verification at claim time; geographic eligibility per local law, with a **no-purchase-necessary alternative entry route where required**; one entry per couple; taxes are the winner's responsibility. Nothing in this section is an offer of a prize.

## 8. Leaving: unlinking & account deletion
- **Leaving a couple.** Either of you can leave at any time, on your own, without the other's agreement and without them being notified. Leaving unlinks you immediately; your former partner keeps the shared space and everything in it, and nothing either of you wrote is deleted. Your shared streak and points stay with that space — a new relationship starts fresh. Your invite code is replaced so an old one can't be used to rejoin. If you are the subscriber, your subscription stays with you. **Once you leave you can no longer access the shared journal**, so save anything you want to keep before you go.
- Account deletion has a **7-day grace period** — signing back in cancels it. After it lapses, your account and authored content are permanently deleted.
- Deletion/unlinking is non-destructive to your partner: they keep the couple space and their own content.
- Signing out or deleting your account clears this device's on-device cache and discards any photos still waiting to upload. Photos that had not finished uploading are not saved.
- We may delete dormant unverified accounts after extended inactivity [DEFINE].
